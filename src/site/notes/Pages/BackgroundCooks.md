# A Cooking Garden
BackgroundCooks was my first Digital Garden and is meant as a recipe book. 

In it I break down recipes into multiple ones whenever a part could be used for more than one recipe. For instance I have a [Pie Crust](https://recipes.backgroundlogic.dev/recipes/pie-crust/) recipe that I use for multiple other recipes and so they just link to the pie crust recipe for that section. 

Some recipes I have created from scratch such as my [Basic Sourdough](https://recipes.backgroundlogic.dev/recipes/basic-sourdough/) and others that I have re-written for my own understanding and ease of reading from other sources, with those sources cited like with [Vedbread](https://recipes.backgroundlogic.dev/recipes/vedbread/) a recipe from Hero's Feast: A D&D Cookbook.

I update it periodically whenever I get into a new recipe, but that recipe usually goes through several revisions before I publish it.
